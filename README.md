# Cuphead + Mega Man SFR Mashup

A Cuphead-hosted boss rush featuring Mega Man Super Fighting Robot's weapons, robot masters, and music.

## What This Is

Play as Mega Man in a Cuphead-style boss arena. Use Mega Man's movement and weapons inspired by Mega Man SFR (Axe Man, Coal Man, Dagger Man, Leaf Man, etc.) to defeat Cuphead bosses. Fight through classic Cuphead stages with Mega Man SFR's iconic weapon swap mechanics and music from the player's installed copy of Mega Man SFR.

## Requirements

- **Cuphead** (Windows PC, any version)
- **Mega Man Super Fighting Robot by MrWeirdGuy** (Windows PC)

Both games must be installed on your PC. This mashup reads content from your own copies—nothing is bundled.

## Installation

1. Install this mod through Melty.
2. Melty will verify you have both games installed.
3. Launch through Melty's Play button.

## Gameplay

- **Character:** Mega Man with dash and jump mechanics
- **Weapons:** Swap between Mega Man SFR weapons (Axe, Coal, Dagger, Leaf, etc.)
- **Bosses:** Classic Cuphead boss patterns and phases
- **Music:** Mega Man SFR robot master themes play during fights
- **Stages:** 1–5 boss encounters per playthrough
- **Solo Play:** Single player, no co-op
